## 2. Tone of voice

Verbatim phrases:

- "Safe, happy learning" (used as a sign-off)
- "There is something powerful about seeing the world come together in one place."
- "Differences are strengths."
- "After a summer away, there is nothing better than seeing the Aquila community back together again."
- "We couldn't be prouder of our students."
- "Success is often found in the small, meaningful moments."
- "Everyone deserves to be seen, heard and empowered to thrive."
- "Step onto our purpose-built campus... designed to spark curiosity and creativity."

**Style:**

- Warm, reassuring and parent-facing: safety and happiness first, then ambition. Polite and professional but conversational, in short clear sentences.
- British spelling (enrol, programme, centre). Say "pupils" or "students"; avoid "kids" in formal speech.
- Use words like belonging, community, close-knit, journey, pathways, visible progress, curiosity, confident, "Students of Determination".
- Avoid hard sell, discount-led pitches, jargon and American spelling.
- The principal speaks in a measured, reflective way (AI used with guidance, no phones during the school day, mistakes as part of learning).

## 5. Admissions

**Process** [tas/admissions/how-to-apply]:

1. Enquiry.
2. School tour (virtual for overseas families).
3. Meet and greet:
   - EYFS and Primary: with the Head of Primary or a phase leader.
   - Secondary: CAT4 test and an interview with the Head of Secondary.
4. Offer by email, accepted within 5 working days, then registration and deposit.
5. Welcome visit and start-up information.

**Rules** [tas/faq]:

- No application fee. Applications are accepted all year.
- Age is counted at 31 August: FS1 turns 4 and FS2 turns 5 in the school year. From Year 3, placement follows the transfer certificate.
- Documents:
  - passports, visas and Emirates IDs for the child and parents;
  - vaccination record;
  - attested transfer certificate;
  - registrar forms.
- First come, first served, with sibling priority. A waiting list operates when a year group is full, and a place offered from it must be accepted within 1 week.
- A device is required in secondary.

**Fees 2026-27** (AED per year, KHDA-approved, paid 40/30/30 by term) [tas/admissions/tuition-fees]:

**Discounts:**

- Sibling: 5% for the 2nd child, 15% for the 3rd, 25% for the 4th, 100% for the 5th. Can't be combined with corporate discounts.
- Corporate: Visa, Fazaa, Emirates, Esaad, HSBC.
- Early bird for paying the full year: 7% by 31 May, 5% by 30 June, 3% by 31 July.
- **Aquila Family Circle referral** (27 Aug–31 Oct 2026):
  - the new family gets 20% off 2026/27 tuition;
  - the referring family gets AED 5,000, then AED 2,000 for each further referral.

**Scholarships:**

- ISP Middle East is offering 100 scholarships worth up to 100% of tuition (announced 15 Mar 2026):
  - Academic Excellence and International Learner: Years 7–10 and 12.
  - Tomorrow's Leaders: Year 12, with 5+ GCSEs at grade 6 or above.
- Requirements: 95% attendance, 2 extra-curricular activities, and a 300–500 word essay. The process runs through an SLT assessment and then the Principal.
- Performing arts scholarships of up to 75%. [tas/admissions/scholarships]
