# Willowbrook School admissions (fictional demo)
Willowbrook is a fictional independent day school. Programs and annual tuition in USD are available through admissions.programs. Fees exclude transport, meals and optional clubs; no discounts or places are guaranteed. Admissions accepts enquiries year-round, subject to a staff review and capacity. Steps: discuss the year group, visit the school, submit an application through the admissions office, then await staff review. Do not upload child documents to this chat. The office explains any required documents through its secure application process. A tour or enquiry does not reserve a school place. Only application.status records confirm current progress; the assistant cannot issue an admissions decision.
